Our team is experienced, well-educated, and passionate about making learning a transformative experience. We excel in the following areas:

  • We Understand the Admissions Process: Many of our team members have studied at world’s most elite universities. We help students highlight their achievements and express them to admissions committees in convincing and compelling ways.   • We Are Selective About Students: Just as students and parents are careful in selecting the right mentors to work with, we are thoughtful about selecting clients who we believe are a good fit for us. As teaching and counseling is an intense and often sustained process, we believe it is important to have clients who share similar values. Student motivation and parent engagement are crucial for success, as we believe that relationships as well as study habits are critical for success. We do not overload our team with large numbers of students to supervise to ensure that each student gets ample attention.
  • We Strive to be Ethical: Our team works hard to ensure that students act in ethical and responsible ways. We do not take exams for students, we do not write student essays, and we attempt to ensure that our students are upstanding citizens.
  • We Value Our Scholarship and Advisement, Not Pure Businessmen: While every company seeks profits, the majority of our staff is invested its in academics and counseling activities. Some of our team has published professionally in academic fields and education while others have explored putting together online courses for the wider public. All of our team is engaged with the learning process and has attended numerous learning and educational training seminars. We treat our mentors well because their work is professional in nature.
  • We Are Culturally Aware: Our team has worked with students from a wide range of countries across Asia, Europe, and America. We have worked with students of various political leanings, sexual orientations, and socioeconomic backgrounds.
  • We Have a History of Success: Our founder and many of our expert team members have placed students into top universities consistently for the past decade. With students regularly gaining admissions to top 50 American colleges – as well as some elite schools outside America – we are proud of our students and our record.

The College Admissions Process:

College admissions in America is a holistic process that seeks to evaluate the academic, extracurricular, and personal qualities of each applicant. While the application itself typically is submitted in the beginning of an applicant’s senior year, assessment of applicants begins as early as grade 9. Below is a chart (in English) of the principal areas assessed in applicants to an American University.

We aim to enroll students from grade 11 or the early fall term grade 12 to help them best prepare themselves for the college admissions process. Our guidance is holistic and aimed to bring out the best qualities in each individual student.

The principal areas of assessment are as follows:

  • High School Grades (Transcripts, grades 9-12)
  • Standardized Test Scores (SAT/ACT/AP/IB/TOEFL)
  • College Application Essays and Activities
  • Alumnus/a Interviews
  • Teacher and Counselor Recommendations
  • Portfolio (optional, unless applying for arts school)

The Boarding School Admissions Process:

Boarding school admissions in America evaluates candidates in a

  • School Grades (Transcripts)
  • Standardized Exam Scores (typically SSAT)
  • English Proficiency (TOEFL)
  • School Counselor Recommendation
  • English and Math Teacher Recommendations
  • Personal Essays
  • Parent Statement
  • School Interview
  • Personal or Special Interest Reference (optional)
